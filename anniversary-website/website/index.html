To Stephany,

First of all, happy five months. I know I made you a website for three months and I could've just changed the number and called it a day, but where's the fun in that?

It's still funny to me that we went to watch Scary Movie and somehow that ended up being the day we started dating and said I love you. Out of all the movies it really had to be that one.

Looking back at the pictures of us, I genuinely love how much of it is just us being us. Sitting on the train, going out to eat, taking stupid pictures, carving pumpkins, and me holding Fluffy like he's my child. It doesn't have to be some huge planned date for me to enjoy being with you.

I know I'm still annoying and I probably won't be retiring from ragebaiting anytime soon. But seriously, I do want to keep getting better at being your boyfriend. I want to listen to you properly, remember the little things, and actually show you how much you mean to me instead of just saying it.

I love learning things about you, even the random things. Your coffee that needs enough sugar to feed an entire family, your obsession with Hamilton, your favorite flowers, and how much you care about animals. I really hope I get to see you become a vet one day. I'm rooting for you.

Thank you for these five months. I don't expect either of us to be perfect, and I know there's still a lot for us to learn about each other. I'm just glad I get to do that with you.

I want more random dates, more pictures, more stupid jokes, and more memories that we can look back on. I hope that one day this website has so many anniversaries on it that the time machine actually needs a search bar.

Happy five months, my love. I love you.

Love, Youness ♡
